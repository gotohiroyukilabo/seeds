# Dermatomyositis / Antisynthetase Disease

## Thesis
Autoantibody-positive dermatomyositis and antisynthetase disease remain dependent on steroids, IVIG, JAK inhibitors and conventional immunosuppressants. Antibody seeds are plausible where pathogenic IgG, IFN, complement or lung inflammation define a subgroup.

## Unmet need
- Refractory skin disease, calcinosis, muscle weakness and ILD drive morbidity.
- Steroid-sparing remains imperfect; IVIG is burdensome and costly.
- Rapidly progressive ILD is high-risk and may require broad immunosuppression.

## Antibody-accessible biology
- FcRn for pathogenic autoantibody lowering.
- IFNAR1 for type I IFN-high skin/muscle disease.
- C5/C5a in complement-mediated tissue injury.
- CXCL10/CXCR3 as IFN-linked recruitment axis; antibody tractability depends on redundancy.

## Biomarker strategy
Myositis-specific autoantibodies, CK/aldolase, skin activity scores, IFN signature, CXCL10, complement activation, HRCT/FVC for ILD.

## Competition
Medium. IVIG, JAK inhibitors, FcRn and IFN-pathway ideas overlap. Differentiation requires autoantibody/IFN-high enrichment or less burdensome dosing.

## Seed-relevant targets
FcRn, IFNAR1, C5/C5a, IL6R, CXCL10/CXCR3.
