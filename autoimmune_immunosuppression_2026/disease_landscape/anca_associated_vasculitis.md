# ANCA-Associated Vasculitis

## Thesis
AAV remains a relapse-prone, immunosuppression-dependent disease. However, the most validated non-steroid node, C5aR1, is already addressed by an oral small molecule; antibody seeds must avoid being a me-too CD20 or complement product.

## Unmet need
- Glucocorticoid toxicity, relapse, renal damage and infection from induction/maintenance therapy remain major problems.
- Rituximab works but relapse and hypogammaglobulinemia occur.

## Antibody-accessible biology
- Pathogenic PR3/MPO autoantibodies; FcRn could reduce IgG autoantibodies.
- B-cell targets CD19/CD20/BAFF may reduce relapse biology.
- Complement C5/C5a is validated, but C5aR1 is already drugged.

## Biomarker strategy
PR3 vs MPO, ANCA titers with caution, complement activation, urinary renal injury markers, B-cell repopulation, IgG levels.

## Competition
High for B-cell depletion and complement. FcRn in relapse/flare or steroid-sparing could be explored, but infection and rebound are concerns.

## Seed-relevant targets
FcRn, CD19, BAFF-R, C5/C5a, CD20.
