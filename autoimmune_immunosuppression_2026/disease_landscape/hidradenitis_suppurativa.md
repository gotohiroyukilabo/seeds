# Hidradenitis Suppurativa / Neutrophilic-Autoinflammatory Overlap

## Thesis
HS is not a classic autoimmune disease, but it is often immunomodulator-, antibiotic- and surgery-dependent with major unmet need. It is useful as a technology stress-test for tissue-selective, masked or bispecific antibodies in inflamed skin.

## Unmet need
- TNF and IL-17 blockade do not control all patients.
- Draining tunnels, pain and surgery need remain substantial.

## Antibody-accessible biology
- IL-36R, IL-1α and neutrophil chemokines have biologic plausibility.
- OSMR may capture epithelial-stromal remodeling and itch/pain/fibrosis biology.
- Complement C5a may drive neutrophil recruitment.

## Biomarker strategy
Lesional transcriptomics, neutrophil chemokines, IL-36 signature, tunnels by ultrasound, CRP, Hurley stage, abscess/nodule counts.

## Competition
High in dermatology; several failures/terminations, including amlitelimab HS terminated and bermekimab HS development terminated. A seed must be biomarker-selected and preferably tissue-localized.

## Seed-relevant targets
OSMR, IL36R, pan-ELR CXC chemokines, IL1A, C5a.
