# IgG4-Related Disease

## Thesis
IgG4-related disease is a compelling antibody seed area because glucocorticoids remain foundational, relapse is common, and plasmablast/B-cell biology is clinically validated.

## Unmet need
- Steroids induce responses but chronic exposure and relapse are major problems.
- Fibrotic organ damage can become irreversible.
- Rituximab is used, but optimal maintenance and relapse prevention remain unsettled.

## Antibody-accessible biology
- CD19 depletion reaches plasmablasts and some plasma-cell continuum more broadly than CD20.
- CD19-FcGR2B co-ligation offers non-depleting inhibition of B cells/plasmablasts.
- FcRn can lower IgG, though whether IgG4 itself is pathogenic versus biomarker varies by organ.

## Biomarker strategy
Serum IgG4, plasmablasts, IgE, eosinophils, complement, organ imaging, relapse history. Patient selection should enrich active inflammatory disease rather than late fibrosis.

## Competition
Rising. Inebilizumab was reported FDA-approved for IgG4-RD in 2025; obexelimab is in phase 3. New seeds need better safety, maintenance convenience or fibrosis-active positioning.

## Seed-relevant targets
CD19, CD19-FcGR2B, FcRn, CD38, BAFF/APRIL.
