# Autoimmune Liver Disease / PSC Overlap

## Thesis
Autoimmune hepatitis remains steroid/azathioprine-dependent, while PSC has no approved disease-modifying therapy. Antibody opportunities exist in gut-liver trafficking and fibroinflammatory axes, but clinical and biomarker risk is high.

## Unmet need
- AIH: steroid toxicity, incomplete response/intolerance to azathioprine, relapse after withdrawal.
- PSC: progressive cholestatic fibrosis, inflammatory bowel disease overlap, no established anti-fibrotic standard.

## Antibody-accessible biology
- CCL24 may address inflammatory fibrosis and has PSC trial experience.
- VAP-1/AOC3 and CCL25/CCR9 are trafficking concepts.
- TL1A could link IBD-like mucosal immunity to hepatobiliary inflammation.

## Biomarker strategy
ALP, bilirubin, ELF/fibrosis markers, MRCP, liver stiffness, IgG, autoantibodies, IBD activity, CCL24/TL1A pathway markers.

## Competition
Medium. Many small molecules in PSC; few antibody successes. A new antibody needs a clear inflammatory-fibrotic biomarker subgroup.

## Seed-relevant targets
CCL24, TL1A, VAP-1/AOC3, CCL25/CCR9, IL6R.
