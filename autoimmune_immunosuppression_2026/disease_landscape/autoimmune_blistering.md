# Autoimmune Blistering Diseases

## Thesis
Pemphigus and bullous pemphigoid are antibody-accessible because pathogenic IgG is central and measurable. FcRn, B-cell/plasma-cell and complement/Th2 axes are plausible; the seed challenge is differentiation in a fragmented, increasingly competitive market.

## Unmet need
- Steroids remain common, especially in bullous pemphigoid.
- Rituximab improved pemphigus but relapse, infection and delayed onset remain issues.
- Elderly bullous pemphigoid patients have high infection and frailty risk.

## Antibody-accessible biology
- FcRn lowers pathogenic IgG.
- CD19/CD20/CD38 target B cells/plasmablasts/plasma cells.
- IL4R and complement may address BP inflammatory itch/blister axis.

## Biomarker strategy
Dsg1/Dsg3, BP180/BP230 titers, eosinophils, IgE, complement deposition, steroid dose, PDAI/BPDAI.

## Competition
Medium-high. FcRn and B-cell therapies are active; a differentiated seed likely needs elderly-safe, reversible IgG lowering or biomarker-selected BP inflammation.

## Seed-relevant targets
FcRn, CD19, CD38, IL4R, C5.
