# Sjögren Disease

## Thesis
Sjögren disease is attractive because many patients still lack disease-modifying therapy, yet the disease has accessible B-cell, FcRn and costimulation biology. The strongest opportunity is not broad sicca treatment, but systemic active, B-cell-high or IgG/FcRn-high disease.

## Unmet need
- Symptomatic glandular therapy does not address systemic disease or progression.
- Steroids and conventional immunosuppressants are used for systemic manifestations, with limited controlled evidence.
- Fatigue, pain and dryness endpoints are noisy; organ-systemic disease may be a cleaner entry.

## Antibody-accessible biology
- BAFF-R/BAFF and CD40L are B-cell activation nodes.
- FcRn blockade may reduce pathogenic IgG and immune complexes.
- CXCL13/CXCR5 may capture ectopic lymphoid follicle biology.

## Biomarker strategy
ESSDAI-active patients; IgG, RF, anti-SSA/SSB, complement, cryoglobulin, CXCL13, BAFF, plasmablasts, salivary gland histology and IFN signature.

## Competition
Increasing: ianalumab phase 3, dazodalibep phase 3, nipocalimab phase 2 completed/breakthrough designation reports. Still, no modality has fully solved fatigue/sicca/systemic heterogeneity.

## Seed-relevant targets
BAFF-R, CD40L, FcRn, CXCL13, CD19-FcGR2B.
