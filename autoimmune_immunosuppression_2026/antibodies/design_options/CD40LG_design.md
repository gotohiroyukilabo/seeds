# CD40LG Antibody Design Options

## Recommended direction
Prioritize an Fc-silent, non-crosslinking CD40L blocker. Consider masked design only if blood safety is clean but systemic immune suppression limits dose.

## Blocking antibody
- MoA: neutralize CD40L-CD40 interaction.
- Benefit: upstream control of T-B/APC costimulation.
- Differentiation: weak unless safety/valency differs from advanced programs.
- Safety risk: platelet activation/thrombosis, infection.
- Developability risk: bivalent binding to trimeric ligand.
- Initial assays: CD40 reporter, primary B-cell activation, dendritic-cell cytokines.
- Kill criteria: platelet activation or no dose-feasible pathway blockade.

## Fc-silent antibody
- MoA: CD40L blockade without FcγR/C1q effector functions.
- Benefit: addresses historical anti-CD40L thrombotic liability.
- Differentiation: safer chronic use in SLE/Sjögren.
- Safety risk: residual ligand/platelet crosslinking independent of Fc.
- Developability risk: Fc mutation stability, immune complex behavior.
- Initial assays: human PRP/whole-blood aggregation, FcγR/C1q panel, CD40L blockade.
- Kill criteria: any coagulation/platelet signal at projected Cmax.

## Bispecific antibody
- MoA: CD40L blockade plus second pathway or tissue-localizing arm.
- Benefit: may reduce dose or enrich tissue effect.
- Differentiation: possible in organ-specific SLE/LN or glandular Sjögren.
- Risk: unintended cell bridging and CMC complexity.
- Kill criteria: no additive biology versus mono or any immune activation.

## ADC
Not recommended. CD40L expression on activated normal T cells/platelets makes cytotoxic payload delivery unsuitable for chronic autoimmune disease.

## Masked antibody
- MoA: inactive in blood, activated by inflamed-tissue proteases.
- Benefit: reduce systemic CD40L/platelet and infection liabilities.
- Risk: incomplete masking or activation in circulation.
- Initial assays: plasma stability, disease-tissue protease activation, recovered potency.
- Kill criteria: blood activation or insufficient tissue activation.

## pH-dependent recycling antibody
Lower priority. May improve PK but does not solve thrombotic biology unless combined with Fc/valency safety.
