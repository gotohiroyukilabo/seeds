# Target Card: CD40 ligand / CD154 / TNFSF5

## Summary
- Disease area: SLE/LN, Sjögren disease, type 1 diabetes/MS-adjacent autoimmunity.
- Target: CD40LG, UniProt P29965.
- Proposed modality: Fc-silent, non-crosslinking CD40L blocker; optional masked inflamed-tissue format.
- Recommendation: High.
- Thesis: CD40L is a strongly causal costimulation node, but only next-generation formats that avoid platelet/Fc crosslinking are seed-worthy.
- Last landscape check: 2026-07-05.

## Scoring
| Axis | Score | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Unmet need | 5 | SLE and Sjögren still require steroids/immunosuppressants in refractory systemic disease. |
| Target biology | 5 | T-B/APC costimulation is upstream of autoantibody and inflammatory amplification. |
| Antibody tractability | 5 | Extracellular membrane/soluble ligand accessible to antibody. |
| Differentiation | 4 | Fc-silent/fragment/bispecific engineering can address historical thrombosis. |
| Safety window | 2 | Platelet biology and infection remain central risks. |
| Biomarker strategy | 4 | B-cell activation, plasmablasts, CXCL13, IFN and CD40L pathway markers. |
| Developability | 3 | Valency, Fc silence and immune-complex behavior are nontrivial. |

## Why antibody?
Small molecules do not easily block a large ligand-receptor interface selectively. Antibody format can tune valency, Fc function, half-life and tissue activation.

## Existing landscape
- Dapirolizumab pegol: phase 2 completed and phase 3 SLE programs listed on ClinicalTrials.gov.
- Dazodalibep: phase 2 completed and phase 3 Sjögren programs listed.
- Frexalimab: phase 2/3 MS and phase 2 SLE/T1D programs listed.
- Historical anti-CD40L antibodies were limited by thromboembolic events; this remains the defining failure mode.

## Risks
- Biology risk: broad immune suppression may be needed for efficacy.
- Safety risk: platelet activation/thrombosis, infection, vaccine response impairment.
- Patient selection risk: costimulation-high signature may not map cleanly to clinical endpoints.
- Competitive risk: phase 3 programs reduce novelty.
- Developability risk: bivalent binding to trimeric ligand and Fc interactions can create crosslinking.

## Kill criteria
- Any platelet activation, aggregation, coagulation or thrombus signal in human blood assays at clinically relevant exposure.
- No inhibition of CD40 signaling in primary B-cell/APC assays at feasible concentrations.
- No biomarker-defined responder segment over existing CD40L programs.
- Infection-risk profile not separable from broad immunosuppression.

## Key experiments
Human whole-blood platelet activation/aggregation; CD40L-CD40 blockade in primary B cells and dendritic cells; immune-complex/FcγR binding panel; SLE/Sjögren patient blood and tissue explant PD assays; cynomolgus tox only after human blood safety is clean.

## Evidence notes
Fact: ClinicalTrials.gov lists dapirolizumab pegol phase 3 SLE, dazodalibep phase 3 Sjögren and frexalimab phase 2/3 autoimmunity studies. Inference: a new CD40L seed must compete on safety engineering and biomarker strategy, not target novelty.
