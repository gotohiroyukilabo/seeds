# Target Card: CCL24 / eotaxin-2

## Summary
- Disease area: Systemic sclerosis, PSC/autoimmune liver fibrosis, eosinophilic-fibrotic autoimmune overlap.
- Target: CCL24, UniProt O00175.
- Proposed modality: Neutralizing IgG with pH-dependent recycling; optional dual chemokine bispecific only if redundancy proven.
- Recommendation: High.
- Thesis: CCL24 is a less crowded extracellular fibrosis-associated chemokine with a plausible biomarker strategy, but clinical status of CM-101 makes failure analysis essential.
- Last landscape check: 2026-07-05.

## Scoring
| Axis | Score | Rationale |

| Unmet need | 5 | SSc/PSC lack curative anti-fibrotic immunomodulation. |
| Target biology | 3 | Human disease association plausible; causality still less proven than B-cell targets. |

## Existing landscape
CM-101 is an anti-CCL24 antibody studied in PSC and systemic sclerosis. ClinicalTrials.gov listed CM-101 SSc phase 2 NCT06210945 as suspended, and PSC phase 2 NCT04595825 as completed at API check.

## Risks
- Biology risk: chemokine redundancy may erase efficacy.
- Clinical risk: suspended SSc trial status could reflect strategic, operational, safety or efficacy issues; reason is unknown from API output.
- Safety risk: eosinophil trafficking and host defense effects.
- Developability risk: soluble antigen sink; high dose if CCL24 levels are high.

## Kill criteria
- CCL24 not elevated or not pathway-active in target patient tissue.
- Neutralization fails to reduce fibroblast activation/eosinophil-monocyte recruitment in patient explants.
- Redundant chemokines compensate fully.
- CM-101 public data reveal class-limiting safety or negative efficacy that cannot be designed around.

## Key experiments
SSc/PSC tissue and serum CCL24 quantification; CCR3 chemotaxis blockade; fibroblast activation co-culture; pH-dependent binding and antigen clearance engineering; biomarker-defined retrospective responder analysis if data obtainable.
