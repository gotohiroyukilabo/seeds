# Executive Summary

## Scope
今回の調査は、自己免疫・自己炎症周辺で、ステロイドまたは免疫抑制剤依存が残る疾患に限定した。前回成果物と混ざらないよう、全成果物は `autoimmune_immunosuppression_2026/` 配下に作成した。

## Disease areas explored
1. SLE / lupus nephritis
2. Sjögren disease
3. Systemic sclerosis
4. IgG4-related disease
5. Dermatomyositis / antisynthetase disease
6. ANCA-associated vasculitis
7. Autoimmune blistering diseases
8. Autoimmune liver disease / PSC overlap
9. Celiac disease / refractory or nonresponsive disease
10. Hidradenitis suppurativa / neutrophilic-autoinflammatory overlap

## Top 3 seed proposals
| Rank | Seed | Proposed first indication | Core modality | Why it made the cut |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | pH-recycling anti-CCL24 | CCL24-high early diffuse SSc or PSC inflammatory-fibrotic subgroup | Neutralizing/pH-dependent IgG | Less crowded fibrosis chemokine axis with biomarker enrichment potential. |

## Important non-selected high-value target
CD40L remains scientifically strong and clinically active. It was not top 3 because dapirolizumab, dazodalibep and frexalimab programs make the space competitive. It should be reconsidered only if the team has a clearly differentiated Fc/valency/masked design with human platelet safety as a first gate.

## Important downgrade after obexelimab review
CD19 x FcGR2B was downgraded from a top seed to a conditional seed. The mechanism is attractive, but obexelimab is already the clinical exemplar of this MoA, with phase 3 programs in IgG4-RD and warm AIHA and phase 2 programs in SLE/MS. IgG4-RD is especially unattractive as a lead wedge because inebilizumab/Uplizna is already approved there. A new CD19 x FcGR2B program should only proceed if an obexelimab-like benchmark is available and the new format shows measurable advantage in geometry, SC developability, biomarker-defined responder segment, or disease positioning.

## Cross-cutting conclusions
- BsAb is most compelling when it creates biology that monospecific antibodies cannot, especially CD19 x FcGR2B inhibitory co-ligation.
- ADC is generally unattractive for chronic autoimmune disease unless the target is restricted to a pathogenic cell state and the safety window is proven early; most candidates here fail that bar.
- Masked antibodies are most relevant for targets with systemic repair or platelet liabilities, such as OSMR or CD40L.
- pH-dependent recycling is most relevant for soluble antigen targets with antigen sink, especially CCL24.

## First experiments across all top seeds
1. Human patient-sample pathway activity and responder segmentation.
2. Primary-cell/explant functional reversal at clinically feasible exposure.
3. Safety assays that match the known class liability before animal efficacy work.
4. Developability triage: aggregation, viscosity, Fc function, cross-reactivity and manufacturability.
