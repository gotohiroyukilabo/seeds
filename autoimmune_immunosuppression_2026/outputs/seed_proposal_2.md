# Seed Proposal 2: pH-Recycling Anti-CCL24

## One-sentence proposal
Develop a high-potency anti-CCL24 antibody with pH-dependent recycling for CCL24-high early diffuse systemic sclerosis or PSC inflammatory-fibrotic disease.

## Disease wall
SSc and PSC remain difficult because current treatment slows selected components but rarely reverses fibrosis. Steroid or broad immunosuppression is limited by toxicity and incomplete anti-fibrotic effect.

## Target validity
CCL24 is extracellular, fibrosis-associated and less crowded than IL-6/B-cell axes. CM-101 provides clinical precedent, but the suspended SSc trial status is a major diligence item.

## Why antibody is necessary
Neutralizing a soluble chemokine with long half-life and tunable antigen clearance is a strong antibody use case. pH-dependent binding can improve soluble antigen sink handling.

## Antibody design
Neutralizing IgG with pH-dependent recycling. Bispecific chemokine blockade only if redundancy mapping shows CCL24 alone is insufficient.

## Differentiation
Biomarker-selected CCL24-high patients plus better PK/PD than a conventional neutralizing antibody.

## Major risks
Chemokine redundancy, unclear causality, slow fibrosis endpoints, unknown reason for CM-101 SSc suspension.

## First validation experiment
Quantify CCL24 in SSc/PSC serum and tissue, then test antibody rescue in patient fibroblast-immune co-cultures and CCR3 chemotaxis assays.

## Go / No-Go
Go if a CCL24-high subgroup shows functional pathway dependence and pH-recycling improves antigen clearance. No-Go if tissue activity is absent, redundancy dominates, or CM-101 data reveal class-limiting failure.
