# CD40LG Clinical History

## Current / recent programs
- Dapirolizumab pegol: ClinicalTrials.gov lists phase 2 completed SLE NCT02804763, phase 3 completed SLE NCT04294667, and phase 3 safety/extension NCT04976322.
- Dazodalibep: phase 2 Sjögren NCT04129164 completed; phase 3 Sjögren NCT06104124 and NCT06245408 active not recruiting; LTE NCT06747949 recruiting.
- Frexalimab: phase 2 MS NCT04879628, phase 3 relapsing MS NCT06141473, nonrelapsing SPMS NCT06141486, SLE phase 2 NCT05039840, type 1 diabetes phase 2 NCT06111586.

## Historical failure / limitation themes
- Safety issue: earlier anti-CD40L antibody development was constrained by thromboembolic events, believed to involve platelet CD40L and FcγRIIa-mediated crosslinking/immune complexes.
- Efficacy uncertainty: SLE phase 2 programs often show biomarker or composite response signals but face noisy endpoints and background immunosuppression.
- Patient selection failure: CD40L pathway activity is not yet a routine companion diagnostic.
- Dosing/PK: fragment/PEG formats may avoid Fc liabilities but require careful exposure and immunogenicity monitoring.

## Implication for seed design
Only Fc-silent, non-crosslinking and platelet-clean designs should advance. A new program needs human blood safety as an entry gate, not a late tox assay.

## Updates to target card
- Risks: residual thrombosis despite Fc engineering, broad immunosuppression, competitive crowding.
- Kill criteria: any platelet activation/coagulation signal; no biomarker-defined clinical niche versus active CD40L competitors.
