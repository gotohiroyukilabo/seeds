# BAFF-R / TNFRSF13C Clinical History

## Current / recent programs
- Ianalumab/VAY736: ClinicalTrials.gov lists phase 3 Sjögren studies NCT05349214 and NCT05350072, Sjögren extension NCT05985915, SLE extension NCT06133972, and diffuse cutaneous SSc phase 2 NCT06470048.
- BAFF ligand precedent: belimumab is approved for SLE/LN but leaves slow onset, incomplete response and relapse/steroid-sparing gaps.

## Failure / limitation themes
- Efficacy insufficiency: BAFF pathway blockade is not uniformly effective; mature plasma cells can survive through APRIL/BCMA niches.
- Redundancy: APRIL, CD40L, IL-6 and tissue survival niches may compensate.
- Safety: infection, hypogammaglobulinemia and vaccine response are predictable on-target risks.
- Patient selection: BAFF level alone may not identify BAFF-R-dependent disease.

## Implication for seed design
The key design question is Fc tuning: Fc-silent chronic antagonist versus calibrated depletion. The new seed must show a measurable advantage over belimumab and ianalumab-like approaches.

## Updates to target card
- Risks: advanced competitor, humoral immune suppression, bypass by plasma-cell niches.
- Kill criteria: no superiority over BAFF ligand blockade or no biomarker-selected responder segment.
