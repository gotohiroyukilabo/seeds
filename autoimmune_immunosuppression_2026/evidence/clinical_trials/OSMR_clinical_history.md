# OSMR Clinical History

## Current / recent programs
No approved OSMR-targeted antibody was identified in this focused check. OSM/OSMR appears less crowded clinically than BAFF, FcRn, CD40L, IL-6 or IL-36R.

## Relevant adjacent failures / lessons
- Fibrosis antibodies have frequently failed when human disease tissue causality was weak or endpoints were slow.
- Broad cytokine blockade can miss tissue-remodeling endpoints if patient selection is based only on diagnosis.
- HS adjacent programs show that plausible inflammatory targets can fail or terminate without tight lesion biomarker selection: ClinicalTrials.gov lists amlitelimab HS NCT06118099 terminated and bermekimab HS NCT04988308 terminated.

## Failure / limitation themes
- Biology risk: OSMR may mark inflamed tissue rather than drive disease.
- Safety issue: OSMR may participate in epithelial/stromal repair; chronic blockade could impair healing.
- Developability: receptor-complex pharmacology and species cross-reactivity may complicate translation.

## Implication for seed design
OSMR is a high-upside discovery seed rather than a clinically de-risked target. It needs strong human lesion/explant validation before animal work dominates decision-making.

## Updates to target card
- Risks: wound healing, broad structural-cell expression, uncertain causality.
- Kill criteria: no OSMR-high lesion subset; repair assays show unacceptable impairment; no feasible masked/tissue-localized design.
