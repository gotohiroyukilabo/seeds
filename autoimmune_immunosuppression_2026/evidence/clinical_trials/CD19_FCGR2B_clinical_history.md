# CD19 x FcGR2B Clinical History

## Current / recent programs
- Obexelimab: ClinicalTrials.gov lists phase 3 IgG4-RD NCT05662241, phase 3 warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia NCT05786573, phase 2 SLE NCT06559163, and phase 2 relapsing MS NCT06564311.
- Published phase 2 signals include IgG4-RD open-label pilot and SLE randomized phase 2 exploration of response by gene pathway co-expression.
- CD19 depletion precedent: inebilizumab is approved in NMOSD and reported approved in IgG4-RD in 2025, validating CD19-positive lineage biology but increasing competition.

## Failure / limitation themes
- Efficacy: non-depleting inhibition may be too subtle for organ-threatening disease.
- Patient selection: B-cell gene-pathway co-expression and plasmablast-high signatures need prospective validation.
- Safety: less depletion does not equal no immunosuppression; infection and Ig changes still need monitoring.
- Competitive/IP: obexelimab-like geometry may limit freedom to operate.

## Implication for seed design
This is promising only if a differentiated geometry, indication focus or biomarker strategy can be created. Otherwise it risks being a follower to obexelimab.

## Updates to target card
- Risks: insufficient efficacy, FTO, target-density dependence.
- Kill criteria: no ex vivo inhibition in patient B cells/plasmablasts; no credible differentiation from obexelimab.
