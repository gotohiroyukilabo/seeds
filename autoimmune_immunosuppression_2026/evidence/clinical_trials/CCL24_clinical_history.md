# CCL24 Clinical History

## Current / recent programs
- CM-101 anti-CCL24: ClinicalTrials.gov lists PSC phase 2 NCT04595825 completed.
- CM-101 systemic sclerosis phase 2 NCT06210945 was listed as suspended in the ClinicalTrials.gov API checked on 2026-07-05.
- Earlier healthy volunteer/NASH-related studies are listed as completed.

## Failure / limitation themes
- Efficacy insufficiency: chemokine redundancy is a major risk; blocking one ligand may not change fibrosis trajectory.
- Patient selection: CCL24-high status is not yet a standard diagnostic enrichment marker.
- Biomarker failure: serum chemokine changes may not prove tissue fibrosis modulation.
- Clinical execution: SSc and PSC endpoints are slow and variable; trial suspension reason is unknown from API output.

## Implication for seed design
Proceed only with a strong translational package: CCL24-high patient identification, ex vivo tissue response, and pH-dependent recycling to handle soluble antigen.

## Updates to target card
- Risks: redundancy, uncertain reason for suspended SSc trial, antigen sink.
- Kill criteria: no CCL24-high functional subgroup; CM-101 data show class-limiting safety/efficacy failure.
