# Clinical History: CCL24 / eotaxin-2

調査日: 2026-07-05

## Scope

直接標的: CCL24 / eotaxin-2。

対象薬剤例: CM-101 / anti-CCL24. Direct publicly accessible failure examples were limited in this quick scan; therefore this memo emphasizes known development uncertainties and chemokine-class risks.

## Clinical failure / limitation patterns

| Category | Observation | Relevance to CCL24 |
|---|---|---|
| Efficacy不足 | No confirmed direct anti-CCL24 efficacy failure found in quick public scan. | Unknown does not equal low risk; clinical efficacy remains unproven until phase 2/3 data mature. |
| Safety issue | CCL24/CCR3 biology is linked to eosinophil recruitment and type 2/host-defense biology. | Chronic neutralization may affect eosinophil biology, allergy/infection balance, or tissue repair. |
| Redundancy | CCL11/eotaxin-1 and CCL26/eotaxin-3 can also signal through CCR3; fibrosis has many parallel mediators. | CCL24-only blockade may be compensated by other chemokines. |
| Patient selection failure | SSc/PSC/fibrotic autoimmune disease is heterogeneous; CCL24-high patients may be a subset. | Need CCL24-high, CCR3-cell-rich, progressive fibrosis enrichment. |
| Biomarker failure | Serum CCL24 may not reflect tissue causal biology. | Tissue expression and functional neutralization are required. |
| Dosing / PK issue | Secreted chemokine targets can create antigen sink; tissue concentration and turnover may require high exposure. | pH-dependent recycling or high-potency neutralization may be needed. |
| Immunogenicity | No specific CCL24 antibody immunogenicity issue identified in quick public scan. | Unknown for novel antibodies or engineered recycling designs. |
| CMC / developability | Conventional soluble-ligand neutralizing IgG is feasible. | pH-dependent designs add engineering/developability risk. |

## Existing drug limitations

- No approved anti-CCL24 drug was confirmed.
- CM-101-like programs may occupy the target space; differentiation requires better indication choice, biomarker selection, or PK/PD design.
- Chemokine redundancy is the central biological limitation.

## Key implications for seed evaluation

- Require tissue-level CCL24 elevation and functional neutralization data.
- Compare against CCL11/CCL26 redundancy early.
- Kill if CCL24 is not causally linked to fibrosis/inflammation readouts or if antigen sink makes practical dosing implausible.
