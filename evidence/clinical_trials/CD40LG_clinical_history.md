# Clinical History: CD40 ligand / CD154 / TNFSF5

調査日: 2026-07-05

## Scope

直接標的: CD40 ligand / CD154 / TNFSF5。

対象薬剤例: historical anti-CD40L antibodies including ruplizumab/toralizumab/BG9588 class; newer agents including dapirolizumab pegol, frexalimab, dazodalibep.

## Clinical failure / limitation patterns

| Category | Observation | Relevance to CD40L |
|---|---|---|
| Safety issue | Earlier anti-CD40L antibodies were associated with thromboembolic events; this is the defining historical class liability. | Any CD40L program must prove platelet-sparing biology before efficacy optimization. |
| Efficacy不足 | Dapirolizumab pegol phase 2 in SLE showed signals but did not create immediate registration-level proof; later phase 3 reports are positive, but full data and replication matter. | Efficacy may require correct background therapy, endpoint, disease activity, and responder subgroup. |
| Redundancy | Blocking CD40L may not fully suppress established plasma cells, innate inflammation, IFN-high disease, or tissue-resident autoimmunity. | CD40L is upstream, but not sufficient for all autoimmune phenotypes. |
| Patient selection failure | Broad SLE/Sjogren populations may dilute benefit if disease is not T-B costimulation-driven at treatment time. | Need plasmablast/Tfh/CD40L-CD40 pathway-high selection or PD enrichment. |
| Biomarker failure | Changes in B-cell/T-cell biomarkers may not map to composite clinical endpoints. | Need pre-specified PD-to-clinical linkage. |
| Dosing / PK issue | Fab/PEG or Fc-silent formats may trade lower platelet risk for shorter half-life, altered tissue exposure, or injection burden. | Need exposure sufficient for costimulation blockade without immune over-suppression. |
| Immunogenicity | PEGylated or engineered formats may require ADA monitoring. | ADA could reduce exposure or create safety concerns. |
| CMC / developability | Valency, Fc function, aggregate control, and immune-complex behavior are safety-critical. | CMC is not cosmetic; it is part of thrombosis risk control. |

## Existing drug limitations

- No approved CD40L blocker in autoimmune disease was confirmed in this quick scan.
- Newer CD40L agents attempt to avoid historical thrombosis via Fab, Fc-silent, or engineered formats.
- Active competition means a new seed must specify why its format is safer or its indication/patient selection is better.

## Key implications for seed evaluation

- Platelet activation, FcγR binding, C1q binding, immune-complex formation, and coagulation markers are hard kill criteria.
- Efficacy should be tested in T-B costimulation-high autoimmune samples, not generic inflammatory assays.
- CD40L remains high-value only if the format is demonstrably non-thrombogenic.
