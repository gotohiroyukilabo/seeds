# Clinical History: IL-36 receptor / IL1RL2

調査日: 2026-07-05

## Scope

直接標的: IL-36 receptor / IL1RL2。

対象薬剤例: spesolimab and other IL-36 pathway agents. For hidradenitis suppurativa, nearby inflammatory biologic failures such as IL-17/IL-1 pathway programs are treated as external caution, not direct IL-36R failure unless stated.

## Clinical failure / limitation patterns

| Category | Observation | Relevance to IL-36R |
|---|---|---|
| Efficacy不足 | Spesolimab is validated in generalized pustular psoriasis flares, but efficacy outside GPP is not established from this quick scan. | Extension into HS or broader autoimmune skin disease is a patient-selection and biology risk. |
| Safety issue | IL-36R blockade affects epithelial/innate inflammatory signaling; long-term infection/barrier safety outside GPP is less clear. | Chronic HS dosing may have a different risk profile than acute GPP flare treatment. |
| Redundancy | HS and autoimmune skin inflammation involve TNF, IL-17, IL-1, IL-23, complement, microbiome, and structural tunnel/scarring biology. | IL-36R blockade may not address non-IL-36 inflammatory or structural components. |
| Patient selection failure | Without IL-36 pathway-high enrichment, broad HS trials may dilute effect. | Lesional biomarker selection is central. |
| Biomarker failure | IL36A/B/G RNA or protein may not predict drug response unless linked to functional ex vivo blockade. | Need functional tissue suppression assay, not expression only. |
| Dosing / PK issue | Skin lesion penetration and chronic receptor occupancy requirements are unknown for new indications. | Need tissue exposure/PD biomarkers. |
| Immunogenicity | No IL-36R-specific immunogenicity issue identified in quick public scan. | Unknown for novel formats. |
| CMC / developability | Conventional receptor-blocking IgG feasible. | Developability risk is lower than biology/indication risk. |

## Existing drug limitations

- Spesolimab establishes IL-36R as druggable, but its strongest public validation is GPP flare treatment.
- HS already has TNF and IL-17 biologics; an IL-36R seed must show why IL-36-high patients are distinct and underserved.
- Nonresponse in HS may reflect tunnels/scarring and multi-pathway disease rather than a single cytokine receptor.

## Key implications for seed evaluation

- Require IL-36-high disease tissue and functional suppression in lesional explants.
- Do not assume GPP success translates to HS or autoimmune blistering disease.
- Kill if IL-36R blockade does not suppress patient tissue inflammatory readouts beyond TNF/IL-17 comparators.
