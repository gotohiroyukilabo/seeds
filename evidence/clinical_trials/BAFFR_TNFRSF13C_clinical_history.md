# Clinical History: BAFF receptor / TNFRSF13C

調査日: 2026-07-05

## Scope

直接標的: BAFF receptor / TNFRSF13C, ianalumab/VAY736 など。

近接経路: BAFF/TNFSF13B, APRIL/TNFSF13, BAFF/APRIL ligand traps。BAFF-R直接阻害の公開失敗例は限定的なため、BAFF/APRIL軸の臨床失敗も外挿リスクとして扱う。

## Clinical failure / limitation patterns

| Category | Observation | Relevance to BAFF-R |
|---|---|---|
| Efficacy不足 | Tabalumab, an anti-BAFF antibody, had phase 3 RA development halted and an SLE phase 3 program stopped after failure to meet the primary endpoint. | BAFF pathway blockade can be biologically rational yet clinically insufficient, especially without responder enrichment. |
| Safety issue | Atacicept, a BAFF/APRIL blocker, had MS studies suspended after increased relapses and new MRI lesions were reported versus placebo. | Broader B-cell/plasma-cell axis modulation can produce paradoxical disease worsening in some immune contexts. |
| Patient selection failure | BAFF-axis responses vary by disease, baseline BAFF, B-cell activation, autoantibody profile, and background therapy. | BAFF-R program should avoid broad unselected autoimmune populations. |
| Biomarker failure | BAFF or B-cell biomarkers may show target engagement without clear clinical benefit. | Must link B-cell depletion/blockade to disease-relevant endpoints, not only PD changes. |
| Redundancy | APRIL, CD40L, IL-21, type I IFN and tissue survival niches can maintain autoreactive B/plasma-cell biology despite BAFF-R blockade. | BAFF-R monotherapy may be insufficient in plasma-cell-dominant or tissue-resident disease. |
| Dosing / PK issue | B-cell depletion and receptor occupancy may require durable exposure; optimal chronic dosing window is uncertain. | Need PK/PD model connecting receptor occupancy, B-cell subsets, Ig levels, and efficacy. |
| Immunogenicity | No specific BAFF-R class immunogenicity issue identified from quick public scan. | Unknown; monitor ADA especially for chronic autoimmune use. |
| CMC / developability | Conventional IgG feasible; Fc design is the main developability choice. | Fc-enabled designs add effector-function variability and safety complexity. |

## Existing drug limitations

- Belimumab validates BAFF biology in SLE/LN but has modest/heterogeneous efficacy and slow onset; it does not eliminate the need for steroids/immunosuppression in many patients.
- BAFF/APRIL dual blockade may be stronger but can broaden immunoglobulin suppression and infection risk.
- Ianalumab has active clinical development; if successful, follow-on BAFF-R projects need clear differentiation in Fc function, patient selection, dosing, or indication.

## Key implications for seed evaluation

- Do not advance a BAFF-R seed on B-cell biology alone.
- Require a disease segment with B-cell/BAFF-R dependence and a clinical endpoint likely to move with B-cell modulation.
- Treat hypogammaglobulinemia, infection, and failure to outperform BAFF ligand blockade as core kill risks.
