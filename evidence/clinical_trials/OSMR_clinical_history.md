# Clinical History: OSM receptor / OSMR

調査日: 2026-07-05

## Scope

直接標的: OSM receptor / OSMR。

対象薬剤例: vixarelimab/KPL-716/BIIB069. OSM ligand-targeting history was searched but direct, well-documented autoimmune failure examples were limited in this quick scan.

## Clinical failure / limitation patterns

| Category | Observation | Relevance to OSMR |
|---|---|---|
| Efficacy不足 | Vixarelimab has phase 2a evidence in prurigo nodularis, but no approved OSMR drug was confirmed; autoimmune/fibrotic indications remain unproven. | Clinical proof in itch/dermatology does not prove efficacy in HS, SSc, or autoimmune fibrosis. |
| Safety issue | OSMR biology may affect epithelial repair, stromal remodeling, wound healing, and barrier responses. | Chronic blockade in scarring/ulcerating skin disease could carry repair risks. |
| Redundancy | Stromal remodeling can be driven by IL-6, IL-13, TGF-beta activation, IL-31, TNF, IL-17 and mechanical/tissue damage loops. | OSMR may be one node in a redundant remodeling network. |
| Patient selection failure | OSM/OSMR-high tissue segment is not yet established for target indications. | Broad all-comer trials may fail if only a molecular subset is OSMR-dependent. |
| Biomarker failure | Tissue OSM/OSMR expression may mark inflammation but not causal dependency. | Need ex vivo rescue/blockade data. |
| Dosing / PK issue | Tissue penetration into fibrotic or tunnelled lesions may be limiting. | Need tissue PD assay rather than serum exposure only. |
| Immunogenicity | No OSMR-class immunogenicity issue identified in quick public scan. | Unknown for novel formats or bispecifics. |
| CMC / developability | Standard IgG feasible, but receptor-complex biology and epitope selection matter. | Blocking OSM only versus OSM/IL-31-family effects changes biology and safety. |

## Existing drug limitations

- Vixarelimab validates antibody tractability of OSMR but does not yet validate the proposed autoimmune/fibrotic indications.
- Differentiation from IL-31, IL-4R, IL-13, TNF, IL-17 and anti-fibrotic strategies must be based on tissue remodeling biology, not just itch/inflammation.
- Direct clinical failure examples for OSMR in autoimmune disease were not found in this quick scan; this is an evidence maturity weakness, not reassurance.

## Key implications for seed evaluation

- Treat OSMR as a biology-risk-heavy, differentiation-rich target.
- Require OSMR-high human disease tissue plus functional suppression of remodeling/fibrotic programs.
- Kill if OSMR expression is only a marker of inflammation or if blockade impairs repair-relevant readouts.
